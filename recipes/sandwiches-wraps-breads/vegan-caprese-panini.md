# Vegan Caprese Panini

## ingredients
* 75 g 1/2 cup cashews, soaked in water for a couple of hours or overnight
* 2 cups water
* 3 heaped tbsp arrowroot powder
* 2 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp onion powder
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* (optional but recommended) 3 tbsp nutritional yeast
* (optional) 1 tbsp miso paste
* 2 panini or ciabatta rolls
* 2 large tomatoes
* 2 handfuls of fresh basil

## steps
1. Add the soaked cashews to a blender with the rest of the ingredients and blend until smooth.
2. Transfer to a saucepan and stir on a medium heat for a couple of minutes until the mixture starts to bubble and turn stretchy.
3. Remove from the heat.
4. Cut the panini rolls lengthways and spread some cashew mozarella on both sides.
5. Cut the tomato into thin slices and assemble onto one side of the panini and top with a few fresh basil leaves.
6. Sandwich the two halves together and either cook in a panini press/grill or place on a dry frying pan on a medium heat and press down, using a spatula, for about 2 minutes on each side.
7. Cut the panini's in half and serve!
