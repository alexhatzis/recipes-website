# Spinach and Cheese Stuffed Cauliflower Patties

## ingredients
* 1 medium cauliflower head
* 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded
* 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, shredded
* 1/2 Tbsp salt
* 1/2 Tbsp pepper
* 1/2 Tbsp red pepper flakes
* 1/2 Tbsp parsley
* 1/2 Tbsp garlic powder
* 1/2 Tbsp onion powder
* A few handfuls of baby spinach

## steps
1. Preheat oven to 400f
2. Rice up cauliflower in a food processor. Put it in a glass bowl and cover it with ceram wrap. Poke 3 holes at the top and microwave for 4 minutes. Let cool completely, about 20 minutes.
3. Dump cauliflower on a clean towel. Wring out over the sink to remove as much moisture as possible. At this point the cauliflower should have a dough-like consistency.
4. Carefully put your cauliflower dough into a mixing bowl and add the 2 eggs, 1/2 a cup of parmesan, and 1/2 cup of mozzarella. Add the salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, parsley, garlic powder, onion powder. Mix with a wooden spoon until everything is incorporated.
5. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Place a heaping 1/4 cup scoop of the cauliflower mixture onto the sheet. Split in two, and pat down each half into a disk. Add shredded cheese and some baby spinach, then cover with the other disk, pressing together to form a stuffed patty.
6. Repeat until there is no more mixture, about 6 patties.
7. Bake for 20 minutes before flipping patties using a spatula. Continue baking for 15 more minutes.
8. Remove from oven and serve hot (not too hot!)
