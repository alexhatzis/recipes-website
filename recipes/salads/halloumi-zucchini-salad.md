# Halloumi & Zucchini Salad

## ingredients
* 200 g Halloumi cheese
* 2 Courgettes / zucchini (medium)
* 1 Avocado (medium)
* 40 g Mixed salad leaves
* 8 Cherry tomatoes
* Juice of half a lemon
* 1 Handful of fresh mint
* 1 Handful of fresh parsley
* 2 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil

## steps
1. Slice the halloumi into 1cm thick slices. Heat a dry non-stick frying pan over a medium heat, then add the cheese. Allow to toast for 2–3 minutes, or until the halloumi is golden-brown. Flip and repeat on the other side. Drizzle with lemon juice and set to one side.
2. Use a speed peeler to peel lengthways down the courgettes. I like do one side till I get near the centre, then flip and repeat. I discard the middle bit thats left. Once complete drizzle with lemon juice and season with salt and pepper. If you want to make it look pretty you can rolls some of the courgette strips in to rosettes.
3. Peel and chop the avocado, half the cherry tomatoes and roughly chop the herbs. Place in a salad bowl with the mixed leaves. Dress with olive oil, a squeeze of lemon juice and season with salt and pepper.
4. Chop the halloumi into bite size pieces before adding to the salad.
