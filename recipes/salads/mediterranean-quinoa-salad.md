# Mediterranean Quinoa Salad

## ingredients
* 1 ½ cups cooked quinoa
* 1 cup chickpeas (canned or cooked)
* 1 ½ cups quartered cherry tomatoes
* ½ cup olives (green, kalamata or black) (halved)
* ½ small red onion (finely chopped)
* 1 ½ cup fresh basil leaves (finely chopped)
* 2 tsp olive oil
* 1 tbsp lemon juice
* 2 tsp red wine vinegar
* 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
* 1 ½ tsp salt (or to taste)
* ⅓ tsp ground black pepper

## steps
1. In a big bowl, combine cooked quinoa, cooked/canned chickpeas, olives, chopped red onion, cherry tomatoes and chopped fresh basil.
2. To make the dressing, simply whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, balsamic vinegar, red wine vinegar, ground black pepper and salt in a small bowl.
3. Add the dressing to the salad and gently toss to combine.
