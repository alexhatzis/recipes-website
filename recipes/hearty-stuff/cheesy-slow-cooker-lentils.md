# Cheesy Slow Cooker Lentils

## ingredients
* 300 g (~ 1 1/4 cups) dried green lentils
* 6 medium mushrooms, (diced)
* 2 bell peppers, (diced (I used yellow and red))
* 400 g tin ((~ 1 1/3 cups) chopped tomatoes)
* 350 ml (~ 1 1/3 cups) vegetable stock
* 100 g (~ 1 cup) grated cheddar cheese
* Salt
* Black pepper

## steps
1. Add the first 5 ingredients (lentils through veggie stock) to the slow cooker, and mix well to combine. Cook on high for 3 1/2 hours, or until the lentils are soft.
2. Add the grated cheese, and mix well until it's completely melted (you can put the lid back on for a few minutes to help it melt, if needed). Season to taste, and serve warm. I garnished mine with a little semi-dried parsley and some finely grated cheese.
