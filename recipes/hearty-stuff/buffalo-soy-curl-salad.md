# Buffalo Soy Curl Salad

## ingredients
* 2 cups Butler's soy curls (soaked in boiling water for 5-10 minutes)
* 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
* 2 tbsp gluten-free flour
* 1 tsp onion powder
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* ¼ tsp poultry seasoning (optional)
* ½ cup hot sauce
* 1 tbsp vegan butter (optional)
* ¼ tsp onion powder
* ¼ tsp garlic powder
* 2½ cups romaine lettuce (or other salad greens)
* ¾ cup carrots (shredded)
* 2 stalks celery (chopped)
* 1 avocado (sliced)
* red onion (thinly sliced (optional) )
* ⅔ cup raw cashews (soaked in hot water for 10 minutes)
* ⅔ cup unsweetened dairy-free milk (more if you want it thinner)
* 1½ tbsp lemon juice
* ½ tsp garlic powder
* ½ tsp onion powder
* ½ tsp chives
* ¼ tsp dill
* ¼ tsp parsley
* ¼ tsp pepper
* ¼ tsp celery salt (or regular salt)

## steps
1. Once the soy curls are done soaking, add them to a strainer and wring out all the water from them.
2. In a bowl, mix nutritional yeast, flour, onion powder, garlic powder and poultry seasoning (if using).
3. Add soy curls into the bowl and mix to cover the soy curls completely.
4. If using an air fryer, cook the soy curls for 10 minutes on 380 degrees, shaking the basket halfway through.
5. If baking, preheat oven to 375 degrees. Cover baking pan with some oil and bake soy curls for 15 minutes flipping them halfway through.
6. If pan frying, add 1-2 TB of oil to a pan and fry until crispy.
7. While soy curls are cooking, divide the salad ingredients into 4 bowls and set aside.
8. Add the vegan ranch dressing ingredients to a high-speed blender and blend until smooth. Set aside.
9. Add hot sauce, vegan butter, garlic powder, and onion powder to a saucepan on low to heat up the sauce.
10. When soy curls are cooked, add them to the buffalo sauce pan and mix until fully covered.
11. Add soy curls to salad bowls and top with the ranch dressing.
