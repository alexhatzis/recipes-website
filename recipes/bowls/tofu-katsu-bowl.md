# Tofu Katsu Bowl

## ingredients
* 1 1/2 – 2 lbs chicken breasts (or sub Firm Tofu)
* salt and pepper (roughly 1 teaspoon each, per pound of chicken)
* 1/2 cup flour (or sub cornstarch or rice flour)
* 2 teaspoons granulated garlic powder (or onion powder)
* 2 eggs, whisked
* 1 teaspoon soy sauce or squirt sriracha (for heat) – both optional
* 1 cup Japanese Panko (American bread crumbs don’t work the same here!!!)
* 1-2 tablespoons sesame seeds
* 1/2 teaspoon salt

## steps
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and ready a sheet pan with an optional wire rack on top.
2. Slice or pound chicken breasts
3. down into 1/2 -3/4-inch thick filets. Season both sides with salt and pepper- this is important. Set aside. See notes for TOFU.
4. Make your Katsu Station gathering 3 wide, shallow bowls
5. In the first bowl, mix flour and garlic powder. In the second shallow bowl, whisk eggs adding a little splash of soy sauce or sriracha (for heat) if you like. In the third bowl (or plate) mix panko, sesame seeds, and salt. Place all three bowls near the stove.
6. Dredge the
7. seasoned chicken first in the flour mixture, then the egg mixture, then coat in panko mixture. Repeat with all and set by the stove.
8. Sear
9. . Heat oil over medium-high heat. Lower the heat to medium and place the dredged chicken in the hot skillet. Turn over when perfectly golden, about 4-5 minutes. Cook 3-4 more minutes until the other side is golden, then place these on a sheet pan in the 350 F oven to finish cooking through while you repeat with the second batch of chicken, adding more oil if necessary. (To keep them extra crispy, you could place these on a rack, set over a sheet pan.) Bake until internal temp reaches 160F at the thickest part of the breast.
10. SERVE
11. Serve as an entree with jasmine rice or and a vegetable. Serve over the Asian slaw or over greens as a salad with our Miso Dressing! Serve as a sandwich with a sourdough bun with mashed avocado (Asian guac) or Sriracha aioli, with Asian Slaw or kimchi.
