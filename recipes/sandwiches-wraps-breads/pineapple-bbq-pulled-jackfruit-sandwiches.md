# Pineapple BBQ Pulled Jackfruit Sandwiches

## ingredients
* 2 cans jackfruit
* ¼ diced onion
* 1/4 cup chopped pineapple
* 4 whole-wheat buns
* 1/4 cup tomato passata
* 1 tbsp tomato paste
* 3/4 T unsweetened pineapple juice
* 1 tbsp ahornsirup
* ¼ tsp rice vinegar
* 1 tsp soy sauce
* 1/4 tsp. garlic powder (onion ,salt, dry mustard powder)
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* 1 tsp molasses
* 1 pinch cayenne pepper or more (depends on you ;))
* 2 medium sized avocados
* 1 Tbsp fresh-squeezed lime juice
* 1/2 tsp. chili powder
* 1/2 tsp. sea salt
* 2 tsp finely chopped green onion

## steps
1. Wash the jackfruit well and cut off the hard part. We only need the soft part. If necessary, remove pits.
2. Preheat the oven to 200 ° C. For the sauce, stir all ingredients in a bowl. Now put the jackfruit in the sauce. And put it on a baking sheet lined with baking paper.Bake it for around 15 min. If you do not have a convection oven it takes a bit longer. Now take the jackfruit with a fork apart. Add the pineapple pieces and bake again for at least 5 minutes in the oven.
3. For the guacamole, place all ingredients except the green onions in a blender and mix well. Finally, stir the green onions under.
4. You can serve the burger as you see in the photos.
