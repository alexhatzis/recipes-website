# Jackfruit Pulled Pork Sandwich

## ingredients
* 4 20 ounce cans of Jackfruit (water drained)
* 2 cups ketchup (organic & make sure it is gluten-free)
* 1/2 cup celery (chopped)
* 1/2 cup white onion (chopped)
* 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
* 1/4 cup lemon juice
* 1/4 cup water
* 1/4 cup coconut sugar
* 1/4 cup molasses
* 2 tablespoons tamari soy sauce or coconut aminos
* 1 tablespoon natural liquid smoke
* 1 tablespoon dry mustard
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
* 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
* 1/4 teaspoon all spice
* 2 tablespoons olive oil (optional)
* 1/2 cup green cabbage (shredded)
* 1/2 cup purple cabbage (shredded)
* 1/4 cup carrots (shredded)
* 1/4 cup vegan mayonnaise
* 8 gluten free hamburger buns

## steps
1. Stove Top
2. Add jackfruit pulled pork ingredients (first 17 ingredients) to large pot. Cover and bring to a boil then turn to heat down to simmer for 45 minutes. With a fork twist and pull the jackfruit apart to shred it. Optional to add the olive oil in.
3. Slow Cooker
4. Add jackfruit pulled pork ingredients (first 17 ingredients) to a slow cooker. Cook on high 4-6 hours.
5. With a fork, twist to pull jackfruit into shreds. Optional to add fresh olive oil and mix in.
6. Cabbage Slaw
7. In a small bowl stir together green and purple cabbage, carrots and mayonnaise until combined.
8. Assemble Sandwiches
9. Toast 8 gluten-free hamburger rolls. Lay them open faced. Using tongs or a slotted spoon, scoop the jackfruit pulled pork, draining extra juices, and add it on top of 8 slices of the buns. Top with coleslaw. Put roll top on and serve!
