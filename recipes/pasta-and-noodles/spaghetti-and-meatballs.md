# Spaghetti and Meatballs

## ingredients
* 1 lb lean (at least 80%) ground beef
* 1/2 cup Progresso™ Italian Style Bread Crumbs
* 1/4 cup 2% milk
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
* 1/4 teaspoon pepper
* 1 small onion, finely chopped (1/4 cup)
* 1 large egg
* Hot cooked pasta, any shape
* Pasta sauce, any variety, heated

## steps
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Line 13x9-inch pan with foil; spray with cooking spray.
2. In large bowl, mix all ingredients just until combined.
3. Shape mixture into 24 (1 1/2-inch) meatballs by rolling into balls between the palms of your hands, using a meat baller, or a cookie scoop.
4. Place meatballs 1 inch apart on a baking sheet with sides.
5. Bake uncovered 18 to 22 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 160°F and meatballs are no longer pink in the center.
6. Serve over pasta and top with your favorite sauce.
