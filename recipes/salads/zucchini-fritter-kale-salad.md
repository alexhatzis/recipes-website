# Southwest Zucchini Fritters with Kale Salad

## ingredients
* 1 Whole Zucchini
* 1 oz Monterey Jack cheese
* 1 medium egg
* 1 1/4 tsp Orange-chili spice blend
* 1/4 cup All-Purpose flour
* 2 3/4 oz Lacinato Kale (about one medium bunch)
* 10 oz Sweet potatoes
* 13 1/4 oz Black beans
* 1 whole Roma tomato
* 3 1/2 oz Cilantro cider vinaigrette with agave

## steps
### Roasting sweet potatoes and beans
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Dice sweet potato into small cubes (between 1/4'' and 1/2'', depending on how crispy you want them. Smaller = crispier). Strain and rinse the black beans.
3. PLace sweet potatoes and beans in a large bowl. Drizzle with 1-2 tablespoons cooking oil. Seasion with salt and pepper, then stir to combine. 
4. Spreat the sweet potatoes and beans out in a single later on a lightly oiled, foil-lined baking sheet. Roast until sweet potatoes are for tender and lightly browned, around 16-18 minutes. Stirring them halfway through can help too.
### Salad prep
5. Remove and discard any thick center stems from lacinato kale. Roll leaves into a large "cigar" and slice across into ribbons, about 1/4'' wide.
6. Dice the roma tomato, around 1/4'' pieces
### Zucchini fritters
7. Trim the ends off of the zucchini and discard. Grate the remainder on larger holes of a box grater onto a clean kitchen towel. Once it is all grated, use the towel to wring the shredded zucchini over the kitchen sink to remove as much moisture as possible.
8. Place zucchini and monterey jack cheese in a bowl (you can reuse the one from the potatoes and beans). Add the 1/4 cup of flour and a teaspoon of oil. Season with the orange-chili spice blend, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine, gently folding and compressing until the mixture is cohesive (1-2 minutes).
9. Heat cooking oil in a large pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add 2-4 large spoonfuls of the zucchini mixture at a time, gently pressing to flatten them. Cook for 4-6 minutes on each side, until lightly browned and crispy. Transfer the fritters to a paper-towel lined plate.
### Assembly
10. Place kale in a second large bowl with some olive oil. Massage the leaves until they soften.
11. Transfer the sweet potatoes and beans to the bowl with kale. Add the tomato, and drizzle with the cilantro cider vinaigrette. Toss to combine. Serve and top with fritters over the top.
