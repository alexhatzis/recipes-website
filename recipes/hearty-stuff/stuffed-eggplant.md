# Stuffed Eggplant

## ingredients
* 1 Mitoyo Eggplant
* 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
* 1 Medium Yellow Onion, diced
* 1 Bell Pepper, diced
* 3-4 Cloves Garlic, minced
* 1 14oz can diced tomatoes
* 2 Tbsp + 2 Tbsp tomato paste
* 2 tsp Cumin
* 1 tsp Oregano
* Optional: Chili powder
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* 1 Trombicino Squash, sliced into rounds

## steps
1. Preheat the oven to 325°F
2. Cut the Mitoyo Eggplant in half. Rub 1 Tbps of the olive oil onto the cut faces, then place face down on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
3. Bake in the oven for 30 minutes.
4. Take the eggplant out and let it cool. Once cooled, scoop out the soft part, being careful not to break the skin.
5. Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the remaining 1 Tbsp olive oil. Add the diced onion and cook for 1-2 minutes.
6. Add the diced pepper and garlic cloves. Cook for 2-3 more minutes, until the onion is turning translucent.
7. Add the scooped-out eggplant, the diced tomatoes, and the tomato paste. Stir together, then add the cumin, oregano, optional chili powder, and salt & pepper.
8. Allow the mixture to cook for a few minutes, making sure it does not become mushy as it will continue cooking in the oven afterwards.
9. Raise oven temperature to 350°F
9. Transfer the mixture into the eggplant skins, face up in a baking dish.
10. Combine 2 cups of water and another 2 Tbsp of tomato paste. Pour around the eggplant in the dish. Submerge the squash rounds around the eggplant.
11. Place back into the oven and bake for 30 minutes.
