# Strawberry Walnut Salad

## ingredients
* 1 cup strawberries (halved)
* 1/2 cup walnuts (toasted)
* 1 cup baby spinach
* 1 cup arugula
* 1/4 cup red onion (finely chopped)
* 1/4 cup feta cheese, crumbled
* 3 tablespoons avocado oil
* 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
* 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
* 1 teaspoon keto honey (can be omitted)
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## steps
1. Make the Dressing
2. In a small bowl, add all the ingredients and whisk until combined.
3. Assemble the Salad
4. In a large plate or in a bowl, add the greens and red onions. Top with strawberries and walnuts.
5. Drizzle the dressing over the salad and serve.
