# Vegan Seitan Bao

## ingredients
* 6 Bao Buns (allow 2-3 bao per person)
* 170 g Seitan Chicken
* 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
* 1 tsp sriracha mayo
* 1/2 tbsp neutral oil (for pan frying the seitan)
* 3 tbsp pickled onions (or kimchi)
* 3 tbsp fresh herbs (such as coriander and mint)

## steps
1. Prepare your steamer to steam the bao. Put in some boiling water. Put the bao into the steamer basket and put the lid on.
2. Shred the seitan chicken (if it isn't already in small pieces)
3. Gently pan fry the seitan chk'n until just crispy on the outside. Add the hoisin sauce.
4. Fry for a minute or so until all combined and warmed through.
5. Remove the bao from the steamer. (carefully!)
6. To serve each bao: Split the bun, then add the Hoisin Seitan Chicken, herbs, pickles and spicy mayo to taste.
