# Creamy Butternut Squash Pasta with Broccoli & Kale

## ingredients
* 1 butternut squash peeled and cut into 1/2 inch cubes or 1 package of pre-cut butternut squash
* 1 full head of broccoli, chopped
* 1 package of gluten free pasta
* 3 cloves of garlic, minced
* 1 to 1 1/2 cups of vegetable broth
* 1/4 cup of almond milk
* 1/4 teaspoon of onion powder
* 2 cups of chopped kale
* salt and pepper to taste

## steps
1. preheat the oven to 375 degrees.
2. place the cubed butternut squash and broccoli on a baking sheet.
3. drizzle with olive oil, and add a generous dash of sea salt, and pepper and roast for 30 minutes until tender.
4. cook pasta according to package directions.
5. add the garlic and a little bit of oil to a pan. sauté the garlic until fragrant.
6. once the butternut squash is tender, set the broccoli aside and add the squash, garlic, 1 cup of the vegetable broth, almond milk, onion powder and another dash of sea salt and pepper to a blender and blend until smooth.
7. add in more vegetable broth if the sauce it too thick.
8. taste test to see if more seasoning is needed.
9. add a drizzle of olive oil to the same pan you cooked the garlic in.
10. add the chopped kale with a dash of sea salt and pepper, and saute until wilted and crispy. set aside.
11. drain the pasta, and then pour the butternut squash sauce over the pasta and mix through.
12. fold in the broccoli and kale.
