# Mushroom Lentil Loaf

## ingredients
### For the Loaf:
* 1 cup cooked lentils
* 2 cups finely chopped mushrooms
* 1 cup rolled oats
* 1 teaspoon olive oil (buy here)
* 1 chopped onion
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon soy sauce
* 1 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1 teaspoon thyme
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 1 tablespoon tomato paste
* 1 tablespoon flaxseed meal
* 2 tablespoons water
* Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
### For the Glaze:
* 1/4 cup ketchup
* 1 tablespoon maple syrup
* 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar

## steps
1. Begin by cooking your lentils in the dutch oven until they are soft and tender. Lentils usually take about 15 to 20 minutes to cook thoroughly. Once cooked, drain and set them aside to cool.
2. In the same dutch oven, heat your olive oil over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onions and saute until they become translucent. Next, introduce the minced garlic and continue to saute until it releases its aroma, ensuring not to burn it. Stir in the chopped mushrooms and cook until they start releasing moisture and develop a lightly caramelized surface.
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the sauted vegetables, cooked lentils, rolled oats, soy sauce, ground cumin, thyme, salt, black pepper, tomato paste, and optional walnuts. Mix with a wooden spoon until all ingredients are well melded. Simultaneously, mix the flaxseed meal with water in a small bowl and set aside for a few minutes to thicken, then incorporate this into your mixture as it acts as a binder.
4. Preheat your oven to 350F (175C). Line a loaf pan with parchment paper, ensuring the edges hang over for easy lifting later. Spoon the mushroom lentil mixture into the pan and press down firmly to compact evenly. In a separate bowl, mix the glaze ingredients: ketchup, maple syrup, and apple cider vinegar. Spread this delightful glaze over the loaf’s surface, forming a thin, even layer.
5. Place the loaf in your pre-heated oven and bake for approximately 45 to 50 minutes, allowing the loaf to firm up and the edges to slightly crisp. Remove the loaf from the oven and let it cool for about 10 minutes before slicing. The cooling period is essential as it allows the loaf to set, minimizing crumbling when served.
